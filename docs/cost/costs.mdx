The **Cost** screen estimates what your agent usage would cost — and, if you're on a flat subscription, how that compares to your plan.

## API-equivalent cost

ClaudeSec aggregates `gen_ai.usage.input_tokens` / `gen_ai.usage.output_tokens` from every span and prices them against a built-in pricing table, broken down two ways:

- **By model** — tokens and cost per model, with a running total.
- **By session** — the same, grouped by trace.

When telemetry doesn't carry a model name, ClaudeSec infers one from the harness and marks the row **inferred**. Models with no pricing data show `$0` and a `?`.

<Warning>
  This is an estimate, not a bill. Prices vary by region, tier, and caching, and the built-in table may lag current rates.
</Warning>

<Note>
  **Experimental.** `GPT-5.x` and other non-Claude pricing figures are estimates, and some spans are priced against an inferred model rather than a reported one — treat non-Claude totals as rough.
</Note>

## Subscription-plan awareness

Most coding-agent users are on a **flat subscription**, not pay-per-token — so a raw dollar figure is misleading. Pick your plan from the selector (saved locally):

| Plan | Flat price |
|------|-----------|
| API (pay-per-token) | — |
| Pro | $20 / mo |
| Max 5× | $100 / mo |
| Max 20× | $200 / mo |

For a flat plan, ClaudeSec shows a **value-vs-plan** panel: your API-equivalent spend, the flat monthly price, and the ratio — so you can see whether your usage is "paying for" the subscription rather than worrying about a per-token bill.

## Other panels

The Cost screen also embeds a **Webhook** configurator and a **Database Health** panel (span usage, retention config, manual prune) for convenience.

Backed by [`GET /api/costs`](/api-reference/costs) and `/api/cost-trend`.
