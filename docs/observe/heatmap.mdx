---
title: "Heatmap"
description: "A 7-day × 24-hour calendar heatmap of agent activity and threat density."
---

The **Heatmap** screen is a calendar heatmap — **7 days of the week × 24 hours of the day** — so you can see *when* your agents are active and when threats cluster.

Each cell counts the spans and threat-spans that fell in that day/hour slot. Color encodes intensity, and you can switch what's shaded:

- **Threat %** — share of spans in the cell that were threats.
- **Threat Count** — raw threat count.
- **Span Count** — total activity.

Two layouts: a **Weekday** grid (Sun–Sat × hour) and a **Last 14 days** calendar view. Hover any cell for the exact counts.

This is the fastest way to spot **off-hours activity** — an agent doing things at 3am is itself a low-severity signal the detection engine raises.

Backed by [`GET /api/heatmap`](/api-reference/activity), which returns `grid[dayOfWeek][hour]` with `maxThreats` / `maxSpans` for scaling.
